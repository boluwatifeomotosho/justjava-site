The situation

Workforce administration typically fragments across payroll spreadsheets, employee records in one system, approvals in email and reporting reconstructed at month-end. The result is duplicated work and thin management visibility.

Why it mattered

Growing organisations need one place where employee data, payroll, workflows and management reporting behave as a single operation rather than a collection of documents.

The engineering challenge

Not building screens. Building a configurable platform that qualifying organisations can adopt without the vendor rewriting the software each time policy, calculations or reporting requirements change.

What the platform covers

Employee administrationPayrollWorkflows & approvalsReportingConfigurationIntegrations

Want the platform itself?

The platform is available to qualifying organisations that want to adopt an existing HR and payroll foundation rather than commission an entirely new system.
