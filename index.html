01 · Understand

We do not quote what we do not understand.

A serious software proposal should not be a guess with a price attached.

Before committing to a complex engagement, JustJava works to understand why the project exists, what success looks like, who will use the system, what it must connect to, what could derail delivery and what actually belongs in the first release.

When important information is unknown, we say so.

Engineering Review

US-024 · Payment reconciliation · Rev 2
Problem understood
Settlement files and bank statements are matched manually by finance, every day.
Solution direction
Automated matching engine with an exception queue for anything that does not reconcile.
Dependencies
Bank statement API access. Settlement file format from the payment provider.
Engineering risks
Duplicate transaction references across providers.
Decisions required · 2
Tolerance for amount mismatches. Who approves exceptions.

The JustJava Engineering Review

For larger or technically complex programmes, our team reviews the requirement before the commercial proposal is finalised. We bring back our understanding of the problem, the proposed solution direction, major dependencies, engineering risks and the decisions still required.

You should understand what you are buying before we ask you to buy it.
