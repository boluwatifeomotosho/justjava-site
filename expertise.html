Turn business expertise into software.

Some organisations already know their operation better than any vendor could. What they need is engineering that turns that knowledge into a scalable software product.

From what your people know to what the product does.

Example · payroll rules
What the team knowsCodified ruleProduct setting

“Overtime is time and a half after 40 hours.”

overtime.after_hours = 40
overtime.multiplier = 1.5

Overtime policy · configurable per client

“Staff pension is 8%, we add 10%.”

pension.employee_rate = 0.08
pension.employer_rate = 0.10

Statutory deductions · updated centrally

“Leave builds up by about a day and a half every month.”

leave.accrual_per_month = 1.5
leave.carry_over_max = 5

Leave engine · rules per grade

“Anything above ₦5m needs the MD to approve.”

approval.threshold = 5000000
approval.role = "MD"

Approval workflows · no code changes

When a client's policy changes, their configuration changes. The software does not get rewritten.
