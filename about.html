About

Engineering should be a craft, not a commodity.

JustJava exists because important software deserves more than developers completing tickets. It needs people who understand the business requirement, challenge weak assumptions, design carefully, engineer reliably, test deliberately and take responsibility for getting the system into operation.

There is a difference between software that was built and software that was engineered.

Why JustJava?

Java shaped the way we think about software.

Structure, maintainability and systems designed to survive beyond their first release.

JustJava has grown beyond a single programming language, but that engineering discipline remains central to the company we are building.

Our mission

To design, engineer and support business-critical software for organisations that cannot afford to get the engineering wrong. One accountable team, from business requirement to dependable operation.

Our vision

A world where important software is engineered as carefully as it is imagined. Where “we are working on it” is not project visibility, and clients understand what they are buying before they are asked to buy it.

Our core values.

  • Clarity before code

    We understand why the project exists before deciding what should be built. Serious software proposals should not be a guess with a price attached. When important information is unknown, we say so.

  • Engineering over shortcuts

    Technical decisions get made for reliability, maintainability and ownership beyond the next demonstration. What survives the second release matters more than what looks good in the first.

  • Visibility by default

    You should never have to chase your software team. Because “we are working on it” is not project visibility.

  • Client independence matters

    Good engineering should create options, not lock-in. Stay because you want to, not because you have to. We would rather earn your next project than trap you in the last one.

Our philosophy

One accountable team.

From understanding the requirement and designing the architecture to development, integrations, quality assurance, launch and long-term care. One engineering programme and one team take responsibility for getting your software into operation properly.
